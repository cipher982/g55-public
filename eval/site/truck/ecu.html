Engine ECU: a torque request on CAN

The M113K's ME 2.8.1 accepts a torque request on CAN at 50 Hz with a 100 ms watchdog, while the ESP keeps traction cuts and brake state. The patch is assembly, built by a pipeline that refuses what it cannot justify, run inside an emulator of the ECU, and written to 4 of 11 flash sectors. Checked with the key on and the engine off; not yet on the road. measured

Limits
