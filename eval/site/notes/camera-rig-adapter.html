What was asked

On the windscreen visor mount (1.80 m above the road, 0.239 m right of the truck's centreline, 8.5 degrees down), shipped N16 places each inner lane line 0.20 to 0.25 m inside the road paint even after shifting by the camera offset, and under-reads speed unless the input picture is warped from 1.78 m to 1.45 m. A picture warp can correct lens rotation because rotating a lens does not change occlusion; a single planar warp cannot correct height and sideways offset across flat road and upright vehicles at the same time. The question was whether a low-rank adapter on the vision weights, merged back into an ordinary FP16 ONNX model file while leaving comma's recurrent policy untouched, aligns lane lines, path and speed on the truck's centreline and holds up in closed-loop simulation and on a newer comma release.

What was done

  • Each model is split into its vision encoder and its recurrent policy. Rank-8 low-rank factors (113 vision weight tensors, 3.30M parameters) are attached only to the vision part; the policy stays frozen, and zero-initialized factors merge back into the ONNX file with 0.0 difference in onnxruntime.
  • Training used 4 dual-camera drives from the visor mount, separated by drive and day from 10 validation drives (60,787 steps at 5 Hz) and 2 locked hold-out drives (2,134 steps). Vision history features were refreshed sequentially along each drive during training so recurrent state stayed matched to the adapted encoder.
  • Supervision combined the driver's recorded path and CAN speed, inner lane lines measured from road paint where near (6 to 10 m) and mid (10 to 14 m) bands agreed within 0.12 m, and a per-column teacher from the shipped model on a 1.22 m road-plane warp with lane lines, road edges and lead-vehicle hypotheses shifted by +0.239 m onto the truck's centreline.
  • Variants on the 4 training drives tested two input geometries (exact camera geometry vs keeping the 1.45 m input warp) and a 3-arm single-seed ablation across per-drive step balance (capping long highway drives at 3,600 steps, about 12 minutes per drive and 1,136 total optimizer updates, vs uncapped at 4,208 updates where one 88-minute interstate drive contributed 84.5 % of trainable steps) and direct open-loop steering lateral acceleration supervision (turn_weight 0.0 vs 1.0 on the uncapped corpus; at 0.0, action[0] also has zero teacher mask). All variants were evaluated across the full length of all 10 unseen validation drives (60,787 steps, 4,552 paint frames), checked with the untouched 2026-11-10 road-paint script (1,384 to 1,529 confidence-filtered frames per model using the shared flat-road ridge geometry), scored on the same 87 dense-source closed-loop simulation windows (435 episodes per policy, 1.0 m and 1.5 m pushes left and right), tested on comma's 2026-11-05 model (f22e06e5_56320) with no code changes, and scored once on the 2 locked hold-out drives.

Against the 1.45 m shipped baseline, the 87-window intervals exclude zero ([+0.6, +4.0] and [+0.6, +6.0]) while the 10-drive intervals touch or straddle zero ([0.0, +4.5] and [-2.0, +6.1]), and mean pushed lane MAE on the default (0.842 m, median 0.350 m) matches the 1.45 m baseline (0.831 m, median 0.362 m) while trailing v1 (0.743 m) and raw stock (0.651 m) on non-recovering tail excursions. Applied unchanged to comma's 2026-11-05 model (f22e06e5_56320, exact geometry, 4 balanced training drives), validation lane error on the 1,800-step gate moved from 0.240 / 0.264 m (model width 2.81 m, wheel on line 8.2 %) to 0.057 [0.043, 0.075] / 0.040 [0.030, 0.049] m (model width 3.28 m vs 3.31 m paint, wheel on line 0.0 %, speed over CAN 0.996 [0.987, 1.003]). Scored once on the 2 locked hold-out drives (2,134 steps, 61 paint frames), the balanced N16 1.45 m adapter gave 0.023 / 0.026 m left / right lane error (against 0.203 / 0.239 m for the 1.45 m shipped baseline and 0.483 / 0.493 m exact), 3.33 m lane width against 3.34 m paint, 0.0 % wheel on line, and 1.013 speed over CAN.

What it does not show

  • Behaviour on the road. Nothing was installed on the truck; the runtime and tablet carry the output-frame tag in bench tests only.
  • Lane-line accuracy on unmarked roads, in rain, or on unlit sections without two-band paint stripes.
  • Whether exact-geometry vision adaptation matches the 1.45 m warp in closed loop when trained on a larger visor-mount corpus or higher rank.
