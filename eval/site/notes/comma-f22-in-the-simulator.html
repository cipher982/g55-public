What was asked

comma released a new driving model, f22e06e5_56320, on 2026-11-05; its scores on the G55's drives are on its release page. N16 (96244b1f_12800) is the model this project builds on. In both, steering is a base policy plus a residual head added at a fixed scale, and N16 recovers more pushes with that head at three times its scale (an earlier note). The question was how the new model compares with N16 in bench8, the closed-loop simulator built here (the model steers a simulated truck while the camera view is redrawn from recorded frames), as shipped and at three times its residual scale.

What was done

  • Four untrained policies on every day window of the validation set (73 windows, 9 drives) and the hold-out set (20 windows, 2 drives): N16 as shipped, N16 at 3x, the new model as shipped and the new model at 3x.
  • Each window was scored unpushed and with pushes of 1.0 and 1.5 m to each side under three push shapes: a yaw doublet, a sideways offset with no yaw, and a 3.2 s slow drift. "Recovered" means the truck returns to the policy's own unpushed path; an episode whose views leave the area the simulator can draw counts as a failure.
  • N16 was given the route, drawn, as its navigation input. The new model has no navigation input.
  • No bar was set before the run: it measures and selects nothing. Paired differences use a 95% bootstrap over drives, then windows.
  • N16's doublet and offset-only results matched the earlier scale run exactly on both sets.

  • As shipped, the new model recovered 7.2 to 11.0 points more than N16 on validation; the doublet interval includes zero. At 3x it recovered 4.8 to 8.2 points more than N16 at 3x, and every validation interval includes zero.
  • As shipped, the new model recovered fewer offset-only pushes than N16 at 3x: -17.5 [-27.8, -6.7] points on validation.
  • Part of the difference is fewer pushed episodes leaving the drawable area: on validation 49.0, 43.5 and 28.1% for N16 against 43.8, 38.7 and 20.9% for the new model (doublet, offset only, slow drift).
  • Unpushed, the new model ends right of the driver's path: median end offset -0.073 m on validation and -0.164 m on hold-out (+ is left), against +0.043 and -0.025 m for N16. It returned to the driver's path in more unpushed episodes: 63.0 against 58.9% on validation, 65.0 against 45.0% on hold-out.

What it does not show

  • Every validation and hold-out drive was recorded before the narrow camera was re-aimed; the truck runs the new aim. Nothing at night or on the locked test.
  • Whether the route input matters: N16 had it and the new model has none.
  • bench8 cannot say whether an excursion that leaves the drawable area would have come back.
  • Nothing about the truck, which runs N16 as shipped.
