# Suite and method changes

The site's log reads this file: one line per change, newest first, `- YYYY-MM-DD: what changed`,
optionally ending in a link. Model releases and scoring runs come from the results files and are
not listed here.

- 2026-11-05: The site follows the releases over time instead of reporting one snapshot: a timeline of every release by model family, a feed of releases, test runs and test changes (also as RSS), one page per test with every release's 95% interval, newest first, and one page per release. A new section, Inside the models, shows which of the 32 squares in the N16 nav model's view move its steering on 4 of comma's segments, and a check of that map. No value changed.
- 2026-10-31: The site is a plain results report: one table per test, each model's mean with its 95% interval and its median, newest release first. The G55 score, the tiers on the page, the findings and the trend charts are gone, and the build refuses any list of models in another order and any combined score. Each results file now records its benchmark revision: protocol geval-1 and, for the G55 closed loop and recovery tests, the simulator (g55-2026-10-31 since the rerun below). In that rerun the comparison with the preceding release was missing for Mountain Dew and made against MD v3 instead of N16 for 6b5b705f, in the G55 closed-loop and recovery tables; both are corrected, and no value changed. [7f1f94f](https://github.com/cipher982/g55-public/commit/7f1f94f)
- 2026-10-31: G55 closed loop and the recovery tests rerun for all 9 models on the current fit of the G55 simulation (steering centre and understeer refitted). Each window now starts from the truck's state in its CAN recording of the 2 s before it instead of a steady start; one window of 98 has no valid CAN there and starts steady. Windows and tests are unchanged. Medians at 2 s are now 8.3 to 12.0 cm (they were 12.0 to 15.3 cm), and 8 of 8 models are closer to the path than lebowski with the paired interval below zero (it was 5 of 8). On the G55 score the resamples no longer separate any model: one tier of 9 (it was 7 models, then Cinque v3 and lebowski). [aebff69](https://github.com/cipher982/g55-public/commit/aebff69)
- 2026-10-31: The leaderboard shows tiers instead of places 1 to 9 and medals. Whole drives are resampled 2000 times, and a model sits in a higher tier only when it beat every model in the lower tiers in at least 95% of the resamples; inside a tier, models are listed newest first. On the G55 score the 9 closed-loop models form two tiers: 7 models, then Cinque v3 and lebowski. The site build now fails if a page prints a place, a medal or a tier the resamples do not support. [dc0d9be](https://github.com/cipher982/g55-public/commit/dc0d9be)
- 2026-10-30: Redesigned leaderboard and ranking system: models are ranked 1..N by a 4-pillar G55 Composite Score (40% closed-loop path keeping at 2 s, 25% disturbance recovery at 2 s, 20% open-loop tracking across all 18 models, 15% closed-loop smoothness) with interactive tabs to re-rank by any individual test or release date, replacing the marginal-median tie-rank that grouped 8 of 9 models at rank 1. Added an interactive per-camera-setup breakdown matrix across all 5 G55 camera hardware setups (`by_group`).
- 2026-10-28: G55 closed loop and the heading and off-centre recovery tests now run on 98 windows from 13 drives and 5 camera setups (they were 18 windows from 4 drives; the braking lead keeps its 2 windows), with results per camera setup beside the pooled number. With 10 or more drives the closed-loop ranking now uses intervals. Drives whose recorded camera calibration was carried over from another drive are left out. The 8 current models rescored.
- 2026-10-28: G55 open loop now runs on 119 windows of 30 s from 12 drives and 5 camera setups (it was 4 windows from 3 drives), with results per camera setup beside the pooled number. Both cameras feed the model; on the V13 setup the part of comma's narrow view the 8 mm lens misses comes from the wide camera. Drives whose recorded camera calibration was carried over from another drive are left out. All 17 models rescored.
- 2026-10-28: The front page is a leaderboard: best first by the G55 closed-loop median, medals for the first three, a toggle for newest first, bars from zero with lebowski's value as a dashed line, recovery and open-loop cells shaded by rank within the column. Colours now mean what a model is: shipped or on master (blue), Hugging Face upload only (orange), lebowski (green), earlier models (grey). Charts over time follow, with the region better than lebowski shaded.
- 2026-10-28: The front page opens with four small G55 charts over time and one line per model, newest first: closed-loop median distance from the recorded path at 2 s as a dot, lebowski included, then the recovery medians and the open-loop lateral error. It was a two-screen chart ordered by windows closer than lebowski. Ranks and intervals still wait for 10 drives. Findings, model summaries and the bars on each model's row use the G55 results; comma's segments stay in the harness check. The G55 clip at the top of the page is removed. Scoring runs in the log are grouped per day.
- 2026-10-27: comma's earlier driving models added, openpilot 0.9.5 (November 2023) to 0.11.1: nine models from Farmville to WMI, each run with its own release's inputs, camera warp and command and checked against comma's CI model replay of that model. Open loop on the G55 and on comma's segments; closed loop not yet. Their float16 feature normalisation runs in float32 here (in float16 it overflowed on some frames under ONNX Runtime). New over-time panel: G55 open-loop lateral error.
- 2026-10-27: The G55 sections lead: the ranking is G55 closed loop at 2 s, ranked with intervals once it has 10 or more drives and until then ordered by windows closer than lebowski. comma's public drives move to a harness check near the bottom; the world-model player moves to the method page.
- 2026-10-27: The closed-loop video is replaced by a player: the world model's 256×128 frames as generated, the paths drawn by the page over them, one view at a time.
